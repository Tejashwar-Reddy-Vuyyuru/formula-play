Formula Play privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

In short: Formula Play has no accounts and no server of its own. What you draw, record or film is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Who this is from

Formula Play is made by Tejashwar Reddy Vuyyuru, an individual developer. This policy covers the Formula Play web app and the Android app that opens it.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account details. The app has no sign-in.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos library or files, other than a file you pick yourself.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app shows no adverts.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app does not measure how you use it.

Microphone

The Sound tools use the microphone only after you tap a button that starts listening, and only if you allow it when your device asks. The sound is analysed on your device to find its pitch and notes. It is not recorded to storage, uploaded or shared. Listening stops when you leave the tool or tap Stop.

Camera and video

The video tool uses the camera only after you start it, and only if you allow it when your device asks. Frames are analysed on your device to follow the moving object. A clip you film or pick is held in memory while you use the tool and is not saved by the app, uploaded or shared.

Motion sensors

When you film with the video tool, the app may read your device's motion sensors to check that the phone is being held steady. The readings are used at that moment and are not kept.

What is kept on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information in your browser's storage on your device, so that it is there the next time you open the app:

  • Your level, your run of days and your best scores in the games.
  • Creations you choose to save: drawings, typed formulas, sounds and tunes, stored as short codes.
  • Your settings: light or dark, and motion effects on or off.
  • Recent results from the video tool.
  • A copy of the app itself, so it opens without a connection.

This information never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time from Menu, then Settings, then “Clear my progress and creations”, or by clearing the app's storage in your device or browser settings.

Links and codes you send

When you use “Send to a friend” or “Copy code”, the app creates a link or a short code that contains the formula of what you made. It may also contain a word you typed for the guessing game, or a score. You choose who receives it. Anyone who has the link or code can open it. The developer does not receive it.

Other services involved

  • Hosting. The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub receives technical information when your device loads the app, such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your device contacts Google's servers to fetch them, which shares your IP address with Google. See Google Fonts privacy information.
  • Google Play. If you install the Android app, Google Play handles the download and may collect information under its own policies.

The developer has no access to the information these services receive.

Children

The app does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
